Purity is a process, not a slogan.

Every bottle goes through a 5-stage multi-barrier purification system.

  1. Reverse osmosisRemoves dissolved solids and impurities.
  2. Activated carbon filtrationRemoves chlorine and organic compounds.
  3. Micron filtrationCatches microscopic particles.
  4. UV sterilizationNeutralizes bacteria without chemicals.
  5. OzonationEnsures freshness to the last drop.

We bottle in an FDA-registered, SQF Level 3 certified facility, the highest level of food safety certification. Our water meets and exceeds FDA 21 CFR Part 129 and 165.110, EPA, California Department of Public Health and IBWA standards.
